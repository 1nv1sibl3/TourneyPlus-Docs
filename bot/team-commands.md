# Team Commands

Command group: `team`

## `team rename`

Changes team name.

Examples:
- `team rename Phoenix Legends`
- `team rename 42 Phoenix Legends`

Can be locked once scoring has started.

## `team transfer`

Transfers team leader to a teammate.

Examples:
- `team transfer @NewLeader`
- `team transfer @NewLeader 42`

Can be locked once screenshot/result progression has started.

## `team optout`

Removes your team from current scrim window (cutoff rules can apply).

Use when your team cannot continue in current cycle.
